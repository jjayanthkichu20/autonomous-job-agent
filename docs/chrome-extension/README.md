# Arya AI Job Hunter — Google Chrome Extension

Autonomous AI agent extension that matches your CV to job postings on any webpage (LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Indeed, etc.), computes real-time ATS match scores, tailors cover letters, and auto-fills applications in 1-click.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Google Chrome (Takes 30 Seconds)

1. **Open Google Chrome** and navigate to:
   ```
   chrome://extensions/
   ```
   *(Or click the **three dots menu ⋮** in Chrome -> **Extensions** -> **Manage Extensions**).*

2. **Enable Developer Mode**:
   - In the top-right corner of the Extensions page, toggle the switch for **Developer mode** to **ON**.

3. **Load the Extension**:
   - Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top-left corner.
   - Select the `chrome-extension` folder:
     ```
     C:\autonomous-job-agent\chrome-extension
     ```
   - (Or from your Desktop mirror: `C:\Users\jjaya\Desktop\autonomous-job-agent\chrome-extension`).

4. **Pin the Extension**:
   - Click the puzzle piece icon (Extensions icon) in the Chrome toolbar.
   - Click the **Pin 📌** icon next to **"Arya AI Job Hunter"**.

---

## ⚡ How to Use the Extension to Apply for Jobs

1. **Navigate to Any Job Posting**:
   - Go to any job listing on **Greenhouse**, **Lever**, **LinkedIn**, **Indeed**, **Workday**, or company career portals.
2. **Instant ATS Analysis**:
   - A floating **"Arya AI Agent"** pill will automatically appear in the bottom-right corner showing your **ATS Match Score** (e.g., *96% Match*).
   - Or click the **Arya icon** in your Chrome toolbar to open the full inspection panel.
3. **1-Click Auto-Fill**:
   - Click **"⚡ Auto-Fill Application Form"**.
   - Arya will automatically detect the form inputs (Name, Email, Phone, LinkedIn, Location, Experience) and inject your verified profile data with calibrated ATS keywords.
4. **Tailored Cover Letter**:
   - Open the **Cover Letter** tab in the popup to view the personalized cover letter generated specifically for the detected company and role.
   - Click **"Auto-Fill Letter into Page"** or **"Copy"**.
5. **Update Target Roles & CV**:
   - In the **Profile & CV** tab, switch between target roles (*HR Executive*, *HR Operations Executive*, *Talent Acquisition Specialist*).
   - Click **"Sync Live"** anytime to pull your latest CV updates from the Arya web truth store.
